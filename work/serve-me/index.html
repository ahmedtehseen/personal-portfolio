The brief

Build around real salon operations, not a simple list of open hours.

Serve Me combined a consumer marketplace with an operations platform for salon owners: booking, employees, services, inventory, POS-related workflows, administration, and accounting requirements in one custom product.

The constraint

The original WordPress direction could not support the required appointment logic or the customized Visma e-conomic integration for Danish VAT and accounting workflows.

My contribution

Architecture, backend core, and delivery.

My work covered system architecture, database design, booking and authentication backends, marketplace search, operations workflows, integrations, deployment, code review, coordination, and client communication.

Engineering focus

Three problems shaped the platform.

01

Availability, not just open hours

The booking engine combined staffing, breaks, leave, bookings, service eligibility, and duration to find a valid continuous slot.

02

National marketplace search

PostGIS-backed distance queries supported location-aware discovery across 6,000+ listings, with maps and clustering keeping the experience usable.

03

Accounting as a product requirement

Visma e-conomic and Danish VAT workflows extended the data and service layers beyond the initial marketplace scope.

04

Two audiences, one system

Consumers needed discovery and booking while salon teams needed operational control after the booking was made.
